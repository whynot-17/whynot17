## Replication definition

The primary phenotype gate is inherited from Phase 8-R: top250 weighted, four-background availability, >=3 positive backgrounds, >=2 positive non-HCT116 backgrounds, positive leave-HCT116-out median rho, shared-matrix empirical q<=0.10, >=4 direction-consistent signature combinations and signature robustness. A strong external claim requires PRISM and/or CTRPv2 in addition to GDSC; GDSC1+GDSC2 alone is labeled cross_GDSC_only.

## Novelty audit and final disposition

The only approved non-oncology high-confidence compound was ciclopirox. A post-ranking literature audit found direct CRC evidence and direct activity in HCT-8/5-FU-resistant CRC, including ROS-mediated PERK-dependent ER-stress cell death ([Qi et al., PMID 32719342](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32719342/); [open-access record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7385140/)). Earlier colon-adenocarcinoma evidence is also available ([PMC2888914](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2888914/)). No direct ciclopirox–OXA-R CRC paper was identified in the targeted exact-name audit, but the existing CRC/chemoresistance evidence makes ciclopirox a Class-B downgrade rather than a novel Drug X. The full audit is in `phase8R2_novelty_audit.md`.

**Final Phase 8-R2 result: 0 novel approved non-oncology Drug X candidates after independent replication and novelty audit.** Ciclopirox is retained as a phenotype-positive comparator; the computational pipeline should now pivot to matched parental/OXA-R screens or a focused wet-lab mini-screen.

## Interpretation boundary

A PRISM/CTRP non-hit is not evidence that a drug is biologically inactive in acquired OXA-R CRC; it may be absent from a platform or lack adequate CRC model coverage. If no approved non-oncology drug survives independent replication, the next computational step is stopped and the project should pivot to matched parental/OXA-R drug-screen data or a wet-lab focused mini-screen.

Raw PRISM/CTRP/GDSC files are local only and are not committed.
