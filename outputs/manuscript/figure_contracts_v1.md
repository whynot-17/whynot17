# Figure 1–5 production contracts (V1)

Global contract: Python/matplotlib only; 190 mm double-column width; editable SVG/PDF; 600 dpi PNG review files; Arial/Helvetica sans-serif stack; every rendered PDF glyph at least 5 pt; no figure-caption titles embedded in the artwork; fixed exposure (teal), tumor (burgundy), normal/control (gray), inflammation (muted orange), and hypothetical-bridge (light teal, dashed) identities.

## Figure 1 — Study design

Core conclusion: MiNP ranked 24th in a data-first molecular screen of 267 environmental chemicals but advanced through multistage actionability prioritization to a human-testable DINP exposure axis represented by urinary MCOP.

Archetype: schematic-led horizontal composite.

Panel map: A, discovery universe and audited workflow; B, full 267-chemical primary ranked screen with MiNP, DINP and MBzP direct labels; C, high-level multistage prioritization and MiNP/DINP-to-MCOP translation boundary.

Evidence hierarchy: B is the quantitative anchor; A establishes unbiased discovery; C explains why molecular rank was not the final actionability criterion and prevents molecule/biomarker conflation. Detailed Top-30 triage is reserved for a dedicated candidate-prioritization figure.

Reviewer risk: do not imply that MiNP was the top-ranked chemical, that CTD nominated MCOP, or that the transcriptomic layer proves a DINP mechanism.

## Figure 2 — Primary NHANES association

Core conclusion: Each doubling of urinary MCOP is positively associated with prevalent CRC in the pooled complex-survey analysis; the estimate is implementation-stable and robust to omitting any single cycle, while cycle heterogeneity remains visible.

Archetype: asymmetric quantitative grid.

Panel map: A, hero pooled estimate; B, independent Python/R agreement; C, pooled plus seven leave-one-cycle-out estimates; D, seven cycle-specific estimates and global interaction test.

Evidence hierarchy: A is primary; B and C validate; D is transparent heterogeneity disclosure.

Reviewer risk: do not describe LOCO fits as independent replications or hide the discordant 2011–2012 estimate.

## Figure 3 — Robustness and exposure shape

Core conclusion: The continuous MCOP–CRC association remains positive across prespecified population, timing, tail, dilution and co-exposure analyses; the spline supports an overall association without evidence of nonlinearity, while quartiles are imprecise.

Archetype: quantitative robustness grid.

Panel map: A, grouped sensitivity forest; B, co-exposure forest; C, survey-weighted restricted cubic spline with pointwise 95% CI; D, secondary weighted quartile estimates.

Evidence hierarchy: A and C are primary; B addresses co-exposure; D is deliberately subordinate.

Reviewer risk: do not claim monotonic categorical dose response or that absence of nonlinear evidence proves linearity.

## Figure 4 — Epithelial-centered remodeling

Core conclusion: CRC-associated PPAR/nuclear-receptor suppression localizes to tumor-derived epithelium, epithelial RELA/STAT3 rises in parallel, myeloid cells show the opposite PPAR/NR direction, and the six-patient external dataset is directionally concordant but underpowered.

Archetype: asymmetric paired-data composite.

Panel map: A, 32 TCGA pairs; B, 36 Census epithelial PPAR/NR and RELA/STAT3 pairs; C, donor-level paired PPAR/NR deltas by compartment; D, six GSE144735 pairs.

Evidence hierarchy: B and C are visual core; A is orthogonal bulk support; D is directional external support.

Reviewer risk: do not label disease-state data as DINP perturbation evidence or overstate the six-patient result.

## Figure 5 — Integrated evidence model

Core conclusion: Three evidence layers converge—DINP-axis nomination, human MCOP–CRC association, and CRC epithelial remodeling—but the DINP-to-epithelial bridge remains hypothetical.

Archetype: restrained conceptual synthesis schematic.

Panel map: A, data-driven discovery; B, human biomonitoring; C, CRC biological state. Solid connectors encode observed evidence relationships; one dashed connector encodes the candidate mechanistic bridge.

Evidence hierarchy: B is the quantitative center; A and C are convergent supporting layers; the dashed bridge is visually subordinate.

Reviewer risk: never use causal verbs for DINP-to-CRC or DINP-to-PPAR relationships; explicitly state the need for prospective and direct perturbational validation.
