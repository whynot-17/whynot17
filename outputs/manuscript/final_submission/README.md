# MCOP–CRC manuscript submission package

## Primary files

- `MCOP_CRC_manuscript_final.docx`: editable manuscript with final Supplementary Table references and the revised Figure 2 legend.
- `MCOP_CRC_manuscript_final.pdf`: review PDF generated from the final DOCX.
- `main_figures/`: Figures 1–5 in PDF, SVG and PNG formats. Figure 2 is the final outcome-blinded actionability figure.

Figures are supplied as separate upload files because the source manuscript does not embed figures.

## Locked scientific boundaries

- The discovery-to-human-screen sequence is 267 core chemicals to 87 eligible chemical–biomarker mappings to 15 unique biomarker tests.
- CRC outcomes remained behind the firewall until the 15-test universe was frozen.
- MiNP is a molecular nominee, parent DINP is the exposure-axis parent, and MCOP is the measured urinary biomarker. These are distinct chemical entities.
- The manuscript does not claim a direct MCOP molecular hit, causality, or mediation.
