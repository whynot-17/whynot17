# Step 10-R full outcome-pool coverage audit

This directory is an additive extension of the locked Step 10-R randomized
five-outcome panel. It screens the same frozen 29-test environmental family
against **all 13 outcomes** that passed the outcome-blinded registry eligibility
rules, including the added asthma, high-blood-pressure, and high-cholesterol
questionnaire outcomes.

The expanded registry and the eligible outcome list were constructed before
association results were loaded. No outcome was replaced, removed, or ranked
using exposure–outcome statistics. The 29-test family and the within-outcome
BH-FDR denominator remain unchanged.

This audit is intended to document coverage of the expanded outcome pool; it
does not create a cross-disease multiplicity family and does not establish
causality, temporal ordering, or independence among disease outcomes.
